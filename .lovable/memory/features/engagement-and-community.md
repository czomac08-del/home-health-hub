---
name: Engagement and community value system
description: Positive framing, data permanence messaging, Home Story timeline, quick check-ins, community impact section, and SaveButtonMessage across the platform.
type: feature
---
- All progress language celebrates what's documented, never emphasizes what's missing
- "You've documented X systems — that's X things future you will thank you for"
- DataPermanenceBanner on Profile page: "Your home's records belong to your home, not just your account"
- HomeStoryTimeline on dashboard: chronological view from system_details, maintenance_history, property_records, warranties
- QuickCheckInButton ("Still working fine ✓") on SystemCard and SystemDetailScreen — logs to maintenance_history
- CommunityImpactSection on Profile: shows record count, civic contributor badge, neighborhood message
- SaveButtonMessage below save buttons: "Every record you add makes ComingHomeIQ more accurate for your neighborhood"
- DiscountPotentialSection uses positive framing: "X systems documented so far" not "Y more to configure"
